# What's changing at Headcount Buddy on November 13

Headcount Buddy is moving to a simple model: everything on the web stays free, and the texting assistant becomes a paid plan. If you've been using HCB before November 13, you get founder pricing.

**On a USTA team using Headcount Buddy?** Your team will have a different path. We'll share the details with your captain soon.

## What stays free

- Creating and organizing events, for anyone
- RSVPs, availability and event pages on the web
- Player reports (beta)
- Seeing open spots and sub opportunities on the web
- Texts with the links you need: new events, RSVP links, changes and cancellations

## What's changing

The texting assistant, where you reply in your own words, ask questions and get instant answers, becomes part of a paid plan. Without a plan, you still get texts with links, and replies get a short message pointing you to the link.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Everything above |
| Agent | $4.99/month | The texting assistant: RSVP and ask questions by text, reminders, and first shot at open spots and sub opportunities by text before they're posted on the web |
| Organizer | [price] | Agent, plus help running your events: headcount updates, nudges to players who haven't answered, and filling open spots |

**Finding subs.** When you ask HCB to find a sub and it does, you pay $3 for that sub, collected with your monthly payment. You pay nothing if no sub is found. Being asked to sub is always free.

## Founder pricing

If you're on Headcount Buddy before November 13, the Agent plan is $2.99/month for your first 3 months, then $4.99/month. We'll text you a reminder before the price changes.

## How to sign up

1. Send your first month by Venmo to **@hcbuddy**, with your phone number in the note.
2. We turn on your plan within [24 hours] and text you to confirm.
3. Pay monthly the same way. Cancel anytime by not paying the next month.

## Questions

**What if I don't sign up?** Nothing breaks. You keep using everything on the web and get texts with links. You won't be able to text the assistant.

**Do I lose my RSVPs or history?** No.

**Why charge for texting?** Texting and the AI assistant are most of what it costs to run Headcount Buddy. The web costs almost nothing, so it stays free.

**Can my organizer pay for me?** [Yes. Organizers can cover plans for their players.]

**How do I stop all texts?** Reply STOP at any time.

Questions? Email [help@headcountbuddy.com](mailto:help@headcountbuddy.com).
