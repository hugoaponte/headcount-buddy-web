# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-30 · **Version:** 0.6 (this policy and the [terms of service](terms) are versioned together)

**What changed in 0.6:** the PixieNet partnership ended on November 30, 2026; on that date we deleted the member details PixieNet sent us, and we will finish deleting the league records it supplied by December 31, 2026. This policy no longer describes the partnership: its section is replaced by "Data from our former partner, PixieNet", which records what we deleted and what we kept. It also changes how we handle requests about playing history: match results and playing history are facts we cannot promise to remove, and you can ask us to review our interpretation and analysis of them. This replaces the earlier option to ask us to stop using your playing history.

**What changed in 0.5:** the PixieNet partnership is ending on November 30, 2026. The section "Teams linked to a partner platform (PixieNet)" says so at the top and stays for the record; from that date no data moves between us and PixieNet about any team, and the deletion described at the end of that section runs from it. Nothing else changes.

**What changed in 0.4:** Headcount Buddy now has charges (see the terms, "Fees and plans"), so we keep billing records — described under "What we collect" and "Retention". We still hold no card or bank details.

**What changed in 0.3:** we now describe how we use publicly available tennis results and ratings for scrimmages, lineups and reports, who else sees those reports, and that a second AI provider (DeepSeek) processes messages.

**Status:** Headcount Buddy is in early development. Features, data practices, and this policy may change as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced via the service info page and, where appropriate, via SMS.

Headcount Buddy ("we", "us") provides an SMS-based assistant that helps amateur sports teams coordinate RSVPs and headcount. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how to control or remove it.

## What we collect

When you (as a player or organizer) interact with Headcount Buddy, we collect:

- **Phone number** — the number you text us from, or the number an organizer enrolled for you.
- **Display name** — provided by your organizer when they add you, or the name you give us.
- **Message content** — the text of SMS messages you send to or receive from us.
- **Consent state** — when you opted in (`START`) and, if applicable, when you opted out (`STOP`).
- **RSVP and attendance data** — your responses to event invitations (yes, no, etc.) and any free-text notes you include.
- **Team membership** — which team(s) you belong to, and your role (organizer, player).
- **Gender** — for Mixed teams, so we can balance Mixed courts. Your organizer gives it to us. For teams that were linked to PixieNet, we kept the gender PixieNet had sent us, and your organizer can change it.
- **Availability and match details** — when you have said you are free to play, and the lineups and scores you or your teammates report to us.
- **Playing history from public sources** — for tennis, we may use publicly posted league results: match results, lineups, team rosters and schedules, the rating levels published with them, and the dynamic ratings that public tennis-results sites compute from them. We get these from public websites. We use only records that are publicly posted, and we never log in to anyone's account to get them. There is no shared ID between our records and those public records, so we match you to yours by name and team; if we have matched you to the wrong person, tell us and we will fix it. This covers players who do not use Headcount Buddy too — for example, opponents named in a match report — and they can use the rights below by emailing us. Headcount Buddy is not affiliated with the USTA and does not obtain data from it.

As the service evolves, we may collect additional data necessary to operate new coordination features. Any new data type will be documented in this policy before we begin collecting it, and material changes will be announced as described under "Changes to this policy" below.

- **Billing records** — the coordination fees and plan charges you incur (which event, which month, the amount), the monthly statements we issue you, the reference on each statement and the date it was paid. Payments are made through Venmo under Venmo's own terms and privacy policy; we see only what Venmo shows a recipient (your Venmo name, the amount and the note). We hold no card or bank details.

We do not collect location data, card or bank details, or any data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

## How we use it

We use this data to:

- Send you SMS messages about your team's events (only after you opt in by texting `START`).
- Track RSVPs and headcount for your organizer.
- Improve the quality of our automated responses through aggregated, de-identified analysis.
- Balance scrimmages, suggest lineups and court order, and write match and scrimmage reports, using your RSVPs and the public playing history above. Where a player has no rating on record, we estimate one and label it as an estimate.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties. **We do not share or sell your phone number, your SMS opt-in/opt-out status, or any related consent data with third parties for marketing purposes.** The only data sharing we do is operational, described in the next section.

## Who we share it with

To operate the service, we share data with:

- **Twilio, Inc.** — our SMS delivery provider. Twilio sees the content and metadata of messages we send and receive on your behalf. See Twilio's privacy policy at twilio.com/legal/privacy.
- **Anthropic, PBC** — provides AI that generates message responses. Message content (yours and ours) is sent to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic does not train its models on this data per their commercial terms. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
- **DeepSeek** — also provides AI that writes message responses and reports. Message content and the information needed for a reply are sent to it for processing. See DeepSeek's privacy policy at deepseek.com/privacy.
- **Players and captains on a team** — match and scrimmage reports name the players on each court, including opponents. Each report goes to the people on that team it is meant for.
- **Your team's organizer(s)** — organizers see their team's roster, message history with their team, and RSVP state.

We do not share your data with anyone else. We do not share it for marketing purposes.

## Data from our former partner, PixieNet

Until November 30, 2026, some USTA league teams subscribed to Headcount Buddy through our partner platform PixieNet, and for those teams we exchanged the coordination data that version 0.5 of this policy described. That partnership has ended. Since November 30, 2026, nothing is shared between us and PixieNet about any team or player, and on that date we deleted the email addresses and PixieNet account ids PixieNet sent us for members of linked teams. We are deleting the league records PixieNet supplied and will finish by December 31, 2026. We kept each member's name, phone number and gender, which the team uses to coordinate; what we hold about them is now what "What we collect" describes for everyone. Data PixieNet holds about you is governed by PixieNet's own policies; ask them directly for a copy or deletion.

## Retention

**Public playing history** — we keep public league records while we coordinate or report on that league, and refresh them from the source. Reports already sent stay with the people who received them.

**Billing records** — statements and the fee and plan charges on them are kept for as long as business and tax rules require us to keep accounting records, even after you leave the service.

We keep your data for as long as you have an active relationship with a team on our service. After you opt out (`STOP`), we keep a record of your opt-out (phone number + opt-out timestamp) indefinitely so we don't accidentally re-message you. If your team's organizer stops using the service or deletes their team, we retain associated data for a reasonable transition period (typically 30 days) before deletion, in case the organizer wants to resume. We will delete other data on request, with the exceptions described there — see "Your rights" below.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Opt out at any time** by texting `STOP` to the team's Headcount Buddy number.
- **Request a copy of your data** by emailing help@headcountbuddy.com.
- **Request deletion of your data** by emailing help@headcountbuddy.com. It may not extend to facts from public league records, such as results and playing history, or to reports already sent.
- **Ask us to correct your data**, including a wrong match to a public league record, by emailing help@headcountbuddy.com.
- **Raise a concern about information we present** by emailing help@headcountbuddy.com. Match results, scores and playing history are facts drawn from public records, and we cannot promise to remove them. Interpretation and analysis, some of it written by AI, can miss nuance; if something concerns you, tell us and we will review it, and we may correct or remove it. This does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.
- **Get help** by replying `HELP` or `INFO` to any message from us.

## Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data: encrypted connections to all services, encrypted-at-rest databases, and limited access for the small number of people who operate the service. No system is perfectly secure; in the event of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you promptly.

## Age

Headcount Buddy is for adults: the service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe we have data on someone under 18, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced via the service info page and, where appropriate, via SMS.
