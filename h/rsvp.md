# Replying to RSVPs

Headcount Buddy is your team's helper — it texts you about upcoming events, and all you have to do is text back. No app to download, no account to log in to. Just reply in plain English and you're done.

---

## The basics: saying yes, no, or maybe

When Headcount Buddy texts you about an event, reply however feels natural. It understands a wide range of answers and maps them to the right response automatically.

**Example — a simple yes:**

> **Headcount Buddy:** Hey! Practice is Sunday at 9 am at Riverside Courts. Are you in?
>
> **You:** Yeah, I'll be there
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Perfect, got you down as a yes for Sunday's practice! ✅

**Example — a no with a reason:**

> **You:** Can't make it this week, I'm traveling for work
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** No worries — I'll let your organizer know. Safe travels! ✈️

**Example — a maybe:**

> **You:** Not sure yet, depends on whether my kid's game runs late
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Got it — I'll put you down as a maybe. Let me know if that changes!

You can always include a reason or constraint in your reply ("I might be 10 minutes late," "I can only make the early slot") and it'll be passed along to your organizer exactly as you said it.

---

## Changing your answer

Changed your mind? Just text in again. There's no form to update, no link to click.

**Example:**

> **You:** Actually I can make it Sunday after all
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Great, updated! You're now a yes for Sunday's practice. 🙌

You can flip from yes to no, no to maybe, or anything in between — just say so and it's done.

---

## Saying "probably" — soft answers are totally fine

You don't have to commit to a hard yes or no. Headcount Buddy understands hedged answers too.

| What you say | How it's recorded |
|---|---|
| "I'll be there" | Yes |
| "Pretty sure I can make it" | Probably yes |
| "I think I'm out" | Probably no |
| "Definitely can't go" | No |
| "Not sure yet" | Maybe |

Your organizer can see all of these, so even a soft answer gives them useful information for planning headcount.

---

## When there are multiple upcoming events

If your team has more than one event coming up, Headcount Buddy will ask which one you mean — or list them out with numbers so you can just reply with a digit.

**Example:**

> **Headcount Buddy:** You've got two events coming up:
> 1. Practice — Tuesday at 7 pm
> 2. Match vs. Lakewood — Saturday at 10 am
>
> Which one are you replying about? Just reply 1 or 2.
>
> **You:** 2 — I'm in
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Got you down as a yes for Saturday's match against Lakewood! 🎾

---

## The 12-hour cutoff

Once an event is less than 12 hours away, you'll receive a notice letting you know the RSVP window has closed. If you haven't responded yet, you'll get a friendly heads-up around that time — just a nudge so your organizer has an accurate count before game time.

---

## If your organizer updates your RSVP

Sometimes your organizer will reply on your behalf — for example, if they're confirming a lineup and know you're in. When that happens, you'll get a quick text letting you know what was recorded, so there are no surprises.

**Example:**

> **Headcount Buddy:** Hey, just a heads up — your organizer marked you as a yes for Tuesday's practice. Reply if anything's changed!

---

## If a sub spot you were offered is already taken

Occasionally you'll be invited to fill in for another team as a substitute. If you get back to Headcount Buddy and that spot has already been filled by someone else, it'll let you know warmly.

**Example:**

> **You:** Yes, I can sub in on Friday!
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Ah, that spot just got snapped up — someone else filled it right before you replied. I'll keep you in mind next time!

---

## Tips for players

- **Any natural reply works.** "Yep," "nope," "I'll try," "probably not" — it all gets parsed correctly.
- **Include your reason if you have one.** Your organizer sees the context ("might be late," "have a conflict") and appreciates it.
- **You can change your answer anytime** before the cutoff — just text again.
- **You never have to install anything.** Headcount Buddy lives in your messages app, right where you already are.
