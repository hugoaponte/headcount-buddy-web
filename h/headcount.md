# Headcount: Is It On?

Headcount Buddy watches your confirmed count around the clock — and when something shifts, it acts before you have to ask. Here's how that plays out, from the first RSVP that tips the balance to the message that says "we're all set."

---

## The moment it matters: a proactive heads-up

You don't have to ask "are we good for Saturday?" Headcount Buddy texts you when the count changes.

**When someone RSVPs and you're suddenly over:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You (organizer)**
> Just got one more RSVP — we have 5 for Saturday's practice (4 spots). Bob said he'd skip if needed — want me to ask him?

**Your reply:**

> Yes, ask Bob to skip.

**Headcount Buddy → You:**

> On it — asking Bob now.

**About a minute later:**

> Done. Bob is sitting this one out. We have 4 for Saturday's practice.

That's the whole flow. You sent two texts. Headcount Buddy handled the rest.

---

## When you're short, not over

The same thing happens in reverse. If RSVPs are coming in below your target — and the event is getting close — you'll hear about it:

> **Headcount Buddy → You (organizer)**
> We have 2 of 4 confirmed for Saturday's practice (36 hours away). Victor said he could fill in if needed, Sara is a maybe. Want me to reach out to them?

**Your reply:**

> Ask Victor if he can make it.

**Headcount Buddy → You:**

> Got it — reaching out to Victor now.

If Victor says yes:

> Victor's in — we have 4 for Saturday's practice.

If Victor can't make it:

> Heads up — Victor can't make it. Still 3 of 4 for Saturday.

---

## Checking the count yourself

You can ask anytime:

> **You:** How many do we have for Saturday?

> **Headcount Buddy:** 3 of 4 confirmed for Saturday's practice. You need 1 more.

> **You:** Who's in?

> **Headcount Buddy:** Confirmed: Sara, Mia, Priya. Plus 2 others I don't have names for yet.

> **You:** Is practice on?

> **Headcount Buddy:** Not quite — 3 of 4 confirmed. Still need one more.

Players can check their own status too:

> **Player:** Am I down for Thursday?

> **Headcount Buddy:** Yep — you're confirmed for Thursday's practice. 3 of 4 spots filled so far.

---

## Asking a player to skip (over count)

If you're over and want to ask someone specific:

> **You:** Ask Sara to sit this one out.

Headcount Buddy checks the current RSVPs, then reaches out to Sara directly. What Sara receives depends on how she RSVPd:

- **If Sara said she'd skip if needed** — she gets a warm, low-pressure message: *"Headcount's tight in a good way for Saturday. We've got 4 without you. Totally fine to take this one off, or stay in — up to you."*
- **If Sara is a firm yes** — she gets a respectful note: *"The organizer needs to reduce the group for Saturday and wanted to check if you might be able to sit this one out. No pressure — just let us know either way."*

You'll hear back either way:

> Sara is skipping — we have 4 for Saturday. All set.

or

> Heads up — Sara would like to stay in. Still 5 of 4 for Saturday.

---

## When nobody volunteered to be flexible

If everyone confirmed as a firm yes and no one flagged that they'd skip if needed, Headcount Buddy tells you and gives you two options:

> We have 6 for Saturday (4 spots) and no one flagged as flexible. Current lineup: Sara, Mia, Priya, Devon, Chris, Alex. Do you want to decide who sits out, or want me to reach out to someone?

You can tell Headcount Buddy who to contact, or handle it yourself:

> Drop Devon — he can sit this one out.

Headcount Buddy records the change and lets Devon know.

---

## Asking a player to fill in (under count)

If you're short and want to reach out to someone specific:

> **You:** Ask Victor if he can come Saturday.

Headcount Buddy checks the current RSVPs and texts Victor. Players who said they could fill in if needed get a message that acknowledges that:

> *"We're one short for Saturday's practice. You mentioned you could fill in if needed — any chance you can make it? No worries if not."*

---

## When headcount resolves on its own

Sometimes RSVPs sort themselves out without any outreach — someone drops, someone joins, and the count lands exactly right. When that happens, you get a quick note:

> Done — we have 4 for Saturday's practice. All set.

---

## The lock window: close to game time

Within 12 hours of an event, RSVPs are locked for players if you have a headcount target set. This protects the count when you've already made your plans.

If a player tries to drop out after the lock kicks in, they're told to contact you directly — and you get a heads-up:

> Heads up — Victor tried to drop out of today's practice but RSVPs are locked. They know to text you if it's urgent.

As the organizer, you can still make changes after the lock. If Victor texts you and you want to update his status:

> **You:** Victor can't make it — mark him as no.

> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — Victor is marked as not coming.

---

## What triggers a headcount alert

Headcount Buddy checks in at a few points before each event — roughly 3 days out, 36 hours out, and 16 hours out — and also reacts whenever a new RSVP comes in. You won't get repeat alerts for the same situation unless something actually changes.

The 16-hour check always goes out if headcount is still unresolved, even if nothing changed since the last update.

---

## How the confirmed count works

Headcount Buddy counts **confirmed players** as anyone who said yes or indicated they're planning to come but would skip if needed. Players who said maybe, haven't responded yet, or said they could fill in if needed but aren't planning on it don't count toward the total — they're candidates for outreach, not confirmed spots.

This means "how many do we have" always reflects the people who are actually planning to show up.
