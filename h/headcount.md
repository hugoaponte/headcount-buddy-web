# Headcount: Is It On?

The moment your event tips over or falls short, Headcount Buddy notices — and starts working on it before you even think to ask.

---

## The proactive moment

You don't have to ask "do we have enough?" The assistant watches every RSVP and tells you when something changes.

**When one more person says yes and you're suddenly over:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You (organizer):**
> "Just got one more RSVP — we have 5 for Saturday's practice (4 spots). Bob said he'd skip if needed — want me to ask him?"

**When someone drops out and you're suddenly short:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "We're down to 3 of 4 confirmed for Saturday's practice (36 hours out). Victor said he could fill in if needed — want me to reach out?"

You reply in plain English. The assistant handles the rest.

---

## Too many for the spots

When confirmed players outnumber your available spots, the assistant flags it right away and tells you who's most flexible.

### If someone flagged they'd skip if needed

The assistant names that person and asks if you want it to reach out.

> **You:** "Yes, ask Bob to skip."
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "On it — asking Bob now."

A moment later, Bob gets a message like:

> **Headcount Buddy → Bob:**
> "Hey Bob — headcount's tight in a good way for Saturday. We've got 4 without you. Totally fine to take this one off, or stay in — up to you."

Because Bob already flagged he'd be flexible, the message is low-pressure and collegial. He's not being cut — he's being given first choice.

**If Bob says yes to sitting out:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "Done. Bob is sitting this one out. We have 4 for Saturday's practice. All set."

**If Bob wants to stay in:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "Heads up — Bob would like to stay in. Still 5 of 4 for Saturday."

Now you can decide your next move — ask someone else, or handle it yourself.

### If nobody flagged they'd skip

The assistant gives you a picture of who's confirmed and offers you two paths:

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "We have 6 for Saturday (4 spots) and no one flagged as flexible. Current lineup: Sara, Mia, and 4 others. Do you want to decide who sits out, or want me to reach out to someone?"

You can either tell the assistant who to ask, or name someone to drop directly:

> **You:** "Drop Sara — she can sit this one out."
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "Done. Sara is off the list for Saturday."

---

## Too few for the spots

When confirmed players fall short of what you need, the assistant flags it and points you toward the best candidates to ask.

Players who previously said "I could fill in if needed" are first on the list.

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "We're at 2 of 4 for Saturday's practice (36 hours away). Victor said he could fill in if needed, Sara is a maybe. Want me to reach out to them?"
>
> **You:** "Ask Victor."
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "On it — reaching out to Victor now."

Victor gets a warm message that acknowledges his earlier signal:

> **Headcount Buddy → Victor:**
> "We're one short for Saturday's practice. Any chance you can make it? No worries if not."

**If Victor says yes:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "Victor's in — we have 4 for Saturday's practice. All set."

**If Victor can't make it:**

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "Victor can't make it. Still at 3 of 4 for Saturday."

You're always in the loop. No message goes to a player without your say-so, and every response comes back to you.

---

## Checking headcount yourself

You can ask about headcount anytime:

> **You:** "How many do we have for Saturday?"
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "4 of 4 confirmed for Saturday's practice at Riverside Tennis Club. You're all set."

Or check whether the event is on:

> **You:** "Is practice on?"
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "Yes — 4 of 4 confirmed for Saturday at 2:00 PM. You're good to go."

Or find out how many more you need:

> **You:** "How many more do we need?"
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "You need 2 more — 2 of 4 confirmed right now for Saturday's practice."

Players can check too. They'll see the total count, their own status, and a breakdown of who's coming.

---

## When you change the plan mid-week

If you reduce courts or adjust the format, tell the assistant and it will immediately check whether headcount still works at the new number.

> **You:** "Let's just use one court Saturday."
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** "Got it — updated to one court (4 spots). We have 5 confirmed right now, so we're one over. Bob said he'd skip if needed — want me to ask him?"

Everything is rechecked in the same conversation. No need to follow up separately.

---

## The lock window

Within 12 hours of an event, players can't make a change that would hurt your headcount — for example, dropping out when you're already right at your number. They'll be told RSVPs are locked and to contact you directly if something came up.

You'll get a heads-up:

> **Headcount Buddy → You:**
> "Heads up — Victor tried to drop out of today's practice but RSVPs are locked. They know to text you if it's urgent."

As the organizer, you can still make changes right up until game time.
