# Cost, access, and who builds Headcount Buddy

## What does it cost?

Right now, nothing. Headcount Buddy is in **alpha testing** and is completely free to use. The only charge you'll ever see is whatever your mobile carrier normally bills for sending and receiving text messages — that's between you and your carrier, not us.

**Beta testing is coming soon, and it will also be free.**

Looking ahead, as more people join and the costs of running the service grow, we expect to introduce paid subscriptions at some point. A few things worth knowing about that:

- You would only ever pay if you actively choose to subscribe — no surprise charges.
- Many alpha and beta testers are likely to keep discounts or free access even after the testing phase ends.

In short: free now, free through beta, and you'll always opt in before spending a cent.

---

## How do I get access?

Headcount Buddy is in early access right now, so we're adding organizers through a waitlist.

**To join, just text ALPHA to the Headcount Buddy number.** That's it — no app to download, no account to create online. We'll be in touch from there.

If you have questions before signing up, or you're not sure whether Headcount Buddy is a good fit for your group, drop us a line at **help@headcountbuddy.com**.

---

## Who builds Headcount Buddy?

Headcount Buddy is one of the flagship products from **Useful Labs**, a company focused on applying artificial intelligence to real, everyday human problems. Hugo Aponte is a co-founder of Useful Labs.

The team behind it are sports organizers themselves — people who've felt the same weekly headache of chasing RSVPs and counting who's actually showing up. That's why the product is built the way it is: no apps for your players to install, no logins, just text messages that actually work.
