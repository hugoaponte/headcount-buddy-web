# Your welcome message from Headcount Buddy

When you join a team that uses Headcount Buddy, you'll get a text message welcoming you. Here's what to know about that message — when it arrives, what it says, and when it won't show up.

---

## When the message arrives

The welcome lands once two things are true at the same time: you're on the team's roster **and** you've texted in to opt in to messages.

Whichever of those happens second is what triggers it.

That means:

- **If your organizer adds you before you've ever texted in**, you won't get a message right away. The welcome waits. The moment you text in and opt in, it goes out.
- **If you've already opted in** (because you're on another team that uses Headcount Buddy), the welcome goes out as soon as your organizer adds you to the new team.

---

## When the message does NOT arrive

There are a few situations where you won't receive one:

- **You've already gotten it for this team.** The welcome is sent once per team, ever. If you opt out and later opt back in to the same team, it won't send again.
- **The team's subscription isn't active.** Headcount Buddy only sends messages for teams with an active account.

---

## What the message says

The welcome tells you which team you're joining and gives you a short orientation to how Headcount Buddy works — what to expect when it texts you, and how to reply.

**What it says depends on whether you're new to Headcount Buddy entirely:**

- If this is your **first team**, you get the full orientation — a friendly introduction to how the assistant works and what kinds of messages you'll see.
- If you're **already on other teams**, the welcome is shorter. You don't need the full rundown again, so you just get a brief note that you've been added to this team.

**If your team is connected to a partner platform**, the message will also explain how Headcount Buddy fits alongside it — so you know what comes from where.

Here's an example of what the message might look like for someone brand new:

> *Hi! You've been added to the Riverside Tennis Scrimmage group on Headcount Buddy. I'm the team's assistant — I'll text you when there's an upcoming game to check if you're in or out, and you can just reply naturally. No app needed. Talk soon!*

And for someone who's already on another team:

> *Hi! You've been added to Riverside Tennis Scrimmage on Headcount Buddy. See you out there!*

---

## What the message is not

The welcome is purely an introduction. It doesn't:

- List upcoming events or games
- Replay any RSVP requests you may have missed while you were being added
- Ask you to do or confirm anything

You'll hear from Headcount Buddy when there's actually something coming up that needs your attention. Until then, you're all set.
