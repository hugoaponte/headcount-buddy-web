# Setting up and changing events

Headcount Buddy runs entirely over text — no app, no login. As an organizer, you create events, make changes, and cancel plans the same way you'd text a teammate: just say what you need. This page walks through exactly how that works, using a scrimmage as the example throughout.

---

## Creating an event

Just text Headcount Buddy the basics: what kind of event, when, and where.

> **You:** Hey, let's do a scrimmage Saturday at 10am at Riverside Park courts
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Got it — scrimmage Saturday at 10am at Riverside Park courts. Want me to text the team for RSVPs?

That's it. The event is on the books.

**Location is always required.** If you forget to include it, Headcount Buddy will ask before saving anything:

> **You:** Scrimmage Sunday at 9am
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Sounds good — where's it happening?

### What kinds of events can you create?

You can schedule a practice, scrimmage, match, game, hitting session, clinic, or anything else. Use whatever word fits your group — Headcount Buddy takes your language at face value.

### Adding an end time

Include an end time if it's useful and Headcount Buddy will confirm the full window:

> **You:** Scrimmage Saturday 10am–noon at Riverside Park
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Scrimmage set — Saturday 10am–noon at Riverside Park. Want me to send RSVPs?

---

## Changing an event

Tell Headcount Buddy what changed. You don't need to restate everything — just give the update.

> **You:** Move Saturday's scrimmage to 11am
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — scrimmage moved to Saturday at 11am at Riverside Park. *(9 players had RSVP'd: 6 yes, 2 maybe, 1 no.)*
> Want me to let everyone know about the time change and ask them to re-confirm?

Headcount Buddy makes the change right away, then checks on your current RSVPs so you have the full picture. It won't ask "are you sure?" — you already decided. If there's something worth flagging (like a headcount that now looks tight), it'll surface that *after* the change is done.

### Notifying players about changes

After a time or location change, Headcount Buddy will offer to re-notify your players and ask them to re-confirm. A time change goes to everyone who responded — including people who said no, since the new time might work for them. A location change goes to players who said yes.

> **Headcount Buddy:** Want me to let the team know the location moved to Lincoln Park and ask everyone coming to re-confirm?
>
> **You:** Yes, go ahead
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — players who RSVP'd yes or maybe will get a heads-up about the new location.

Players receive a message like this once you give the go-ahead:

> *"Hey! Quick update — Saturday's scrimmage moved to Lincoln Park (was Riverside Park). Can you still make it? Reply yes, no, or maybe."*

If you include "let the players know" or "tell everyone" in the same message as your change, Headcount Buddy will notify them right away — no need to wait for a separate follow-up prompt.

## Adjusting headcount rules for one event

Your team has default headcount rules — how many courts, how many players per court. You can override these for a single event without changing your team defaults.

> **You:** For Saturday's scrimmage, let's do just one doubles court
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Updated — Saturday's scrimmage is set for one doubles court (4 spots). Your usual team defaults are unchanged.

If changing the court setup affects how many players you currently have confirmed, Headcount Buddy will flag it:

> **Headcount Buddy:** Heads up — you have 7 confirmed for what's now a 4-spot court. Want me to help sort that out?

## Canceling an event

Headcount Buddy takes cancellations seriously, so it always reads back the event details and asks you to confirm before anything is canceled.

> **You:** Cancel Saturday's scrimmage
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Just to confirm — you want to cancel the scrimmage on Saturday at 10am at Riverside Park?
>
> **You:** Yes
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Canceled. Want me to text the team to let them know it's off?

If you have more than one upcoming event and it's not clear which one you mean, Headcount Buddy will list them and ask — it won't guess.

### Notifying players about a cancellation

Once the event is canceled, players are automatically notified. By default the notice goes to everyone except players who explicitly said no. If you want to include those players too, just say so:

> **You:** Tell everyone — even the people who said no
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Got it, I'll include them too.

You can also give a reason and it'll pass that along:

> **You:** Courts got flooded
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Got it — I'll let the team know Saturday's scrimmage is off due to the courts being flooded.

Players receive something like:

> *"Hi! Saturday's scrimmage at Riverside Park is called off — courts got flooded. See you next time!"*

## Letting players create their own events

By default, only you can create events for your team. If you'd like to give players the ability to set up informal sessions on their own — a pickup hitting session, an extra practice — you can turn that on:

> **You:** Let players schedule their own practices, up to 2 courts
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — your players can now create their own practice events, up to 2 courts each.

You stay in the loop: Headcount Buddy will give you a heads-up whenever a player creates one. Players can never create matches or official games — that stays with you.

You can also cap how many events a single player may create per week, so no one accidentally floods the schedule:

> **You:** Let players create scrimmages too, but no more than 2 per player per week
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — players can create practices or scrimmages, up to 2 events each per rolling week.

You can also choose whether players are allowed to have Headcount Buddy text the roster for RSVPs on their behalf — by default that stays with you:

> **You:** Let players send out RSVPs for their own events
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — players who create an event can now have me text the roster for RSVPs.

## Delegating an event to a teammate

Need someone else to manage a specific event — handle the headcount, get the alerts, make changes? You can hand it off:

> **You:** Give Saturday's scrimmage to Jamie
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Done — Jamie is now managing Saturday's scrimmage and will get all the headcount updates for it. You're still the team organizer.

This only reassigns that one event. It doesn't change Jamie's role on the team.

---

## Quick tips

- **Always include a location.** Headcount Buddy will ask if you leave it out — saves a round-trip to include it upfront.
- **One message can do multiple things.** "Schedule a scrimmage Saturday at Riverside — actually make it a practice" is handled in one step, not two.
- **Changes go through right away.** No confirmation screen. If there's a consequence worth knowing about, Headcount Buddy tells you after it acts.
- **Cancellations always get a confirmation check.** This is the one place Headcount Buddy pauses — canceling the wrong event is a hard thing to undo.
