# START, STOP, and INFO — Your Headcount Buddy Keywords

Three keywords put you in control of your texts with Headcount Buddy. You can use them anytime, from any phone, with no app required.

---

## The three keywords at a glance

| Text this | What it does |
|---|---|
| **START** | Opts you in — messages can now flow both ways |
| **STOP** | Opts you out — no more messages until you say START again |
| **INFO** or **HELP** | Sends you a quick reminder of what Headcount Buddy is and how to reach us |

That's it. Every keyword is exact, case-insensitive, and must be sent on its own — "stop please" won't work, but "STOP", "stop", or "Stop" all will.

---

## START — joining up

**Text START to +1-425-552-8339.**

Here's what that looks like:

> **You:** START
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** You're in! Headcount Buddy will text you about games, scrimmages and other events once your organizer adds you to a group. Msg frequency varies, msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

If your organizer has already added you to a roster when you text START, you'll see your team mentioned right in the reply — for example:

> **Headcount Buddy:** You're in! Headcount Buddy will text you about games, scrimmages and other events. You're a player on Lake Tigers. Msg frequency varies, msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Or if you organize a team yourself:

> **Headcount Buddy:** You're in! Headcount Buddy will text you about games, scrimmages and other events. You're an organizer of Lake Tigers. Msg frequency varies, msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

**Important:** Headcount Buddy will never text you first. You have to send START before any messages can reach you. Organizers share the number with their players out of band — over group chat, email, or word of mouth — and each player opts in on their own.

If you've previously stopped and want back in, just text START again. You'll pick up right where you left off.

## STOP — opting out

**Text STOP to +1-425-552-8339 at any time.**

> **You:** STOP
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** You're opted out of Headcount Buddy. Reply START to resubscribe.

A few things to know:

- **It works immediately.** Once you've texted STOP, no further messages will be sent to you.
- **Your carrier enforces it.** Mobile carriers enforce opt-outs at the network level, so the block is real — not just a setting in our system.
- **You can always come back.** Text START whenever you're ready and you'll be opted in again right away.

Headcount Buddy also recognizes a range of opt-out words beyond STOP — including QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, and others — so the usual keywords you'd expect all work.

If you text something after opting out, here's what you'll see:

> **Headcount Buddy:** You're opted out of Headcount Buddy. Reply START to resubscribe. (Your carrier may also keep messages blocked until you do.)

## INFO and HELP — quick reminders

Not sure who's texting you, or need the support email handy? Text INFO or HELP.

> **You:** HELP
>
> **Headcount Buddy:** Headcount Buddy helps sports groups coordinate games, scrimmages and other events. Reply START to opt in, STOP to opt out. Problem or feedback? Just text or email help@headcountbuddy.com. Msg & data rates may apply. [terms] [privacy]

You'll get the same reply whether you're opted in, opted out, or texting Headcount Buddy for the first time. It always includes the support email so you have an off-platform way to reach us no matter what.

## If Headcount Buddy seems quiet

Occasionally the assistant may be briefly unavailable. If that happens, you'll see something like:

> **Headcount Buddy:** I'm feeling a bit under the weather right now. I'll reply to your message as soon as I can. You can also email help@headcountbuddy.com. Thanks for hanging in!

Just wait a moment and try again — or drop a note to **help@headcountbuddy.com** if it's urgent.
