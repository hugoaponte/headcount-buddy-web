# What's changing at Headcount Buddy

Until now the team behind Headcount Buddy has covered every cost of running it: the servers, the text messages and the AI. To keep the service going and keep adding to it, we need to cover at least our basic costs from here on.

In short:

- **The web stays free.**
- **From Nov 16, 2026:** $1 when you play an open-group event.
- **From Dec 1, 2026:** $2.99 a month to text the assistant.

You still get texts with the links you need. Join a plan in 2026 to be a founder member: half price for the first 3 months.

## What stays free

Using the web stays free:
- Creating and organizing events
- RSVPs, availability and event pages
- Player reports (beta)
- Sub offers and open spots: they still reach you by text, with a link to claim the spot
- Texts with the web links you need for the above

## From Nov 16, coordination fees

Saying yes to an open-group or pickup event costs $1 once the event is held. Cancelled events don't carry a coordination fee; a yes you don't show up for still gets a fee.

Organizers pay nothing on their own events within the group. USTA team events cost nothing within the team this season. On a team with the **Captain Intel** plan, the team's events carry no coordination fee — any kind of group.

Finding a sub from outside the group costs $3 per sub found, paid by whoever asked. The sub pays the usual $1 coordination fee.

Event Unlimited add-on removes the coordination fee, more below in the plans section.

## From Dec 1: the Agent plan

The texting assistant, where you reply in your own words and get instant answers, becomes the Agent plan. Without it you still get texts with links, and a reply gets a short message pointing you to the right link.

## From Dec 1: Reports

Your own post-match report and the scrimmage reports stay free. Your team's post-match report comes with the Agent plan. 

Captains can take **Captain Intel** for the team: the Lineup report before each match, the Weekly report, every post-match report and the lineup tool — $24.99/month per team, $12.49 for the first 3 months in 2026, paid by the captain. It includes the Agent plan for the team's captains and co-captains, and no coordination fees on the team's own events. The captain can share the reports with anyone.

## How to pay

At the end of each month we text you a statement: your next month's plan, plus the month's coordination fees, with one Venmo link to **@hcbuddy** (the note is filled in for you — keep it, it is how we match your payment). Pay within 5 days. Your next month appears on your statement; pay it to continue. The first statement comes **Nov 30**: coordination fees since Nov 16. If you prepaid December, that is already covered.

To start a plan on Dec 1, Venmo **@hcbuddy** the first month with your phone number in the note, any time before then; we confirm by text within a day, and from then on your next month appears on your statement.

## Questions

**What if I don't pay?** Your schedule page and your RSVPs stay, and the web stays free without a monthly plan. What stops, five days after a statement with coordination fees on it arrives unpaid: a new yes — by text or on the web — until the fees are settled, and the assistant if you had the plan.

**Do I lose my RSVPs or history?** No.

**Why charge?** Texting and the AI assistant are most of what it costs to run Headcount Buddy. The web costs almost nothing, so it stays free.

**On a USTA team?** Your team's events stay free this season, and the texting assistant becomes a paid plan on Dec 1, when your season is mostly done. If the team would like to chip in for the season, any amount to @hcbuddy with the team name in the note is welcome — optional, always.

**How do I stop all texts?** Reply STOP.
